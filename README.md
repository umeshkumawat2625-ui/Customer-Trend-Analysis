📖 Overview
Understanding consumer purchasing habits is crucial for optimizing product offerings, targeted promotional campaigns, and subscription models. This project performs comprehensive Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) on retail customer shopping data using Python, SQL, and Power BI.

It evaluates demographic segments, purchasing frequencies, spending habits across categories, and the influence of discounts and promo codes on total revenue.

🎯 Key Business Questions
Demographic Profiling: How do purchase amounts vary across age groups, gender, and geographic locations?

Subscription & Loyalty: Are subscribed customers spending more on average compared to non-subscribers?

Discount & Promo Impact: How significantly do discounts influence overall sales volume and order values?

Category Performance: Which product categories and item types drive the highest revenue and repeat purchases?

Payment & Shipping Dynamics: What are the most preferred payment methods and shipping types among high-value shoppers?

🛠️ Tech Stack & Tools
Data Processing & Analysis: Python (pandas, numpy), SQL

Data Visualization: Python (matplotlib, seaborn), Power BI

Documentation & Reporting: Jupyter Notebook, MS PowerPoint, PDF

💡 Key Analysis & Insights
Data Preprocessing: Cleaned raw customer transaction records, checked missing values, created spending categories, and transformed customer demographics.

SQL Analytics: Applied complex aggregations, window functions, and subqueries in customer_behavior_sql_queries.sql to isolate high-value customer tiers.

Python EDA: Performed hypothesis testing and correlation analysis between purchase amounts, review ratings, and previous purchases in Customer_Shopping_Behavior_Analysis.ipynb.

📊 Dashboard & Reports
Interactive Dashboard: Open customer_behavior_dashboard.pbix in Power BI Desktop to explore dynamic drill-downs by age group, region, category, and payment channel.
